- **Milestone payments:** Payments for completed and approved milestones are generally non-refundable.

- **Undelivered work:** Where payment has been made for work that has not been performed, the amount attributable to that undelivered work may be refundable, depending on the circumstances and the applicable agreement.
- **Scope changes:** Additional features, revisions, integrations, or requirements outside the agreed project scope may require additional payment and are not automatically covered by the original project fee.

## 1.3 Prebuilt Software Systems: 14-Day Refund Policy

Devrious offers prebuilt software systems and digital software products that may be purchased or licensed for use by customers.

We provide a **14-day refund period for eligible prebuilt software systems that do not function as intended**, subject to the conditions described in this section.

### 1.3.1 Refund Eligibility

To qualify for a refund under this policy:

- The refund request must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.
- The customer must provide a reasonable description of the problem, including the affected features and the circumstances in which the issue occurs.
- The reported problem must relate to a material failure of the software to perform according to its advertised features, documented specifications, or agreed functionality.
- The customer must reasonably cooperate with Devrious in investigating the issue and, where appropriate, testing a fix or workaround.
- The problem must not be caused primarily by unauthorized modifications, unsupported environments, incompatible third-party services, incorrect configuration, misuse, or failure to meet the published system requirements.

### 1.3.2 Refund Assessment

Once we receive a refund request, our team will review the reported issue and may request additional information or reasonable access to a test environment to reproduce the problem.

Where appropriate, we may first attempt to resolve the issue by providing technical support, configuration assistance, a correction, or a software update.

If the software has a material defect that prevents it from functioning as advertised and we cannot reasonably resolve the issue, the customer may qualify for a refund.

We will communicate the outcome of the assessment to the customer.

### 1.3.3 What Is Not Covered

The 14-day refund policy does not automatically apply to:

- Custom software or website development projects.
- Bespoke modifications, integrations, or additional development services.
- Third-party products, subscriptions, hosting, domain registrations, or other external services purchased separately, subject to the applicable provider's terms and legal requirements.
- Problems arising from unsupported environments, unauthorized modifications, misuse, or customer-controlled infrastructure.
- Requests based solely on a change of mind, a change in business requirements, or a preference for different features, where the software otherwise functions as advertised.

These exclusions do not override any refund rights that may apply under the law or any separate written agreement.

### 1.3.4 After the Refund Period

After the 14-day period, refunds for prebuilt software systems are generally not available unless required by applicable law or agreed upon by Devrious.

However, customers may still contact our support team to report defects or request assistance under any applicable support, warranty, maintenance, or service agreement.
